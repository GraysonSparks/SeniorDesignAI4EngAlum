Support Auburn’s Materials Engineering Program
Since 1965, Auburn’s Materials Engineering program has empowered students to blend engineering expertise with scientific curiosity to understand why materials behave the way they do. From water bottles to rocket engines, everything begins with materials. That is why materials engineering is the foundation of innovation across countless industries. Your support ensures Auburn continues to graduate materials engineers who will design better, more sustainable materials for tomorrow’s technologies and drive advancements that improve lives and transform global industries.
